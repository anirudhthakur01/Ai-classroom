Task 9

AI Study Planner

Tell the AI your subjects and exam date, and get a day-by-day timetable with focused 50-minute study slots — weighted by your priorities.

How this works
  1. List your subjects, one per line. Add :1 to :5 to set priority — higher priority means more slots.
  2. Set your daily study hours, start date and exam date.
  3. The AI builds 50-minute slots from 09:00 onwards for each day up to the exam.
