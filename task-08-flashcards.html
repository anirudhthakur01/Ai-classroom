How this works
  1. Paste study text (definitions like "X is Y" work best) or load the sample.
  2. Click ✨ Generate Flashcards — the AI pulls out key questions and answers.
  3. Use Prev / Next to browse, 🔀 Shuffle for random order, and click the card to flip.
