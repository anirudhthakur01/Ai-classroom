Task 7

AI Doubt-Solving Chatbot

Stuck on a concept? Ask your question and get a simple, step-by-step explanation — with conversation history so follow-up doubts stay in context.

How this works
  1. Pick a subject, then type your doubt and press Enter or Send.
  2. The last 12 messages are sent as context, so you can ask follow-up questions like "give me an example".
  3. Connect your own key with the 🔑 button for full live-AI explanations.
