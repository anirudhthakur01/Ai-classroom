Task 4

AI Mind Map Generator

Paste a syllabus or topic outline and get an interactive mind map. Click any node to get a simple explanation of that concept.

Tip: drag to pan, scroll to zoom, click any node for an explanation.

How this works
  1. Paste any syllabus, chapter outline or topic list — one item per line.
  2. Use # headings or indentation (2 spaces per level) to show hierarchy.
  3. Click Generate Mind Map — the AI converts it into a clickable tree.
  4. Click any node to get a simple explanation of that concept.
